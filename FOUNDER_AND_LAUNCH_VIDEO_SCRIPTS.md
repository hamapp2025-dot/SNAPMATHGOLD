# SnapMath Founder And Launch Video Scripts

## Important app rule

Right now the app uses:

- one `intro` video for both English and Arabic
- one `welcome` video for both English and Arabic
- one hero video per lesson for both English and Arabic

So the safest launch format is:

- Arabic main voice
- short English subtitles
- strong equations and visual motion
- very little on-screen text near the bottom

If you want separate Arabic and English videos later, the app can be extended for that, but this is the fastest launch-safe path now.

## Founder package

### 1. Founder portrait

Use this for the welcome and profile founder card.

Checklist:

- square crop
- at least `1200x1200`
- face centered
- chest-up framing
- dark or neutral background
- simple black, navy, charcoal, or white top
- clean expression: calm, confident, warm

### 2. Founder intro video

Purpose:

- first-launch trust
- founder presence
- clear Jordan Grade 12 positioning

Best length:

- `6` to `10` seconds

Arabic master script:

`أنا حمزة الفساطلة. بنيت SnapMath Academy حتى يفهم طالب الأردن الرياضيات خطوة بخطوة ويحل بثقة في التوجيهي.`

Shorter Arabic version:

`أنا حمزة الفساطلة. هنا نفهم رياضيات التوجيهي خطوة بخطوة ونبني ثقة حقيقية للامتحان.`

English subtitle:

`I built SnapMath Academy so Jordanian students can understand Tawjihi math step by step and solve with confidence.`

Shot plan:

1. `0-2s` Medium close-up. Eye contact. Strong first frame. No camera movement.
2. `2-6s` Speak the line clearly. Add subtle app logo or gold accent.
3. `6-8s` End on a still confident look. Leave `0.5s` of clean breathing room for the cut.

On-screen text:

- Arabic top line: `رياضيات التوجيهي خطوة بخطوة`
- English subtitle below center: `Step-by-step Tawjihi math`

### 3. Founder welcome video

Purpose:

- tell the student what to do next
- feel personal and motivating
- transition into study, not sales

Best length:

- `8` to `14` seconds

Arabic master script:

`أهلاً بك. ابدأ بالدرس، ثم ثبّت الفكرة بالأمثلة والتدريب اليومي. هدفنا فهم واضح وثقة حقيقية في الامتحان.`

Shorter Arabic version:

`أهلاً بك. ابدأ بالدرس ثم التدريب اليومي، وخذ كل يوم خطوة واضحة نحو التوجيهي.`

English subtitle:

`Start with the lesson, lock the idea with examples and daily practice, and build real exam confidence.`

Shot plan:

1. `0-3s` Warm medium shot, slight smile, direct eye contact.
2. `3-8s` Speak the instruction clearly.
3. `8-12s` Hold the last line, then end cleanly for the CTA button screen.

On-screen text:

- Arabic top line: `ابدأ بالدرس ثم التدريب`
- English subtitle: `Start with the lesson, then practice`

### 4. Easiest launch shortcut

If you are short on time:

- record only `founder-intro.mp4`
- use that same clip for both intro and welcome first
- then add a separate `founder-welcome.mp4` later

This works because the app already falls back from welcome to intro when the welcome clip is missing.

## Recording setup

Use this for both founder clips:

- iPhone rear camera or a strong phone camera
- `1080x1920`
- `30fps`
- tripod
- eye-level framing
- window light or soft front light
- quiet room
- no echo
- no loud music during recording

Audio tips:

- speak slightly slower than normal
- pause between sentences
- keep one full second of silence before and after each take
- record `3` takes per script

## Fast edit workflow

Best fast stack:

- filming: iPhone Camera
- editing: `CapCut`
- audio cleanup if needed: `Audacity` or `Adobe Podcast`
- compression if needed: `HandBrake`

CapCut workflow:

1. Create a `1080x1920` project.
2. Import the raw clip.
3. Cut silence and mistakes.
4. Apply light color correction only.
5. Add subtitles and keep them above the bottom safe area.
6. Add a very soft gold accent or SnapMath logo only if it looks premium.
7. Export as `H.264 MP4`.

Export target:

- resolution: `1080x1920`
- codec: `H.264`
- frame rate: `30fps`
- bitrate: roughly `8-12 Mbps`

## Lesson hero format

Best length:

- `20` to `35` seconds

Best structure:

1. `Hook`
2. `Core visual rule`
3. `One fast worked example`
4. `Practice handoff`

Use very little talking-head footage in lesson heroes. These should feel visual and premium.

## Lesson scripts

### `u1-l1-hero.mp4`

Title:

- Arabic: `الباقي والعامل في خطوة واحدة`
- English subtitle: `Remainder and factor in one move`

Core formula:

- `P(a) = remainder`
- `If P(a) = 0, then (x - a) is a factor`

Arabic voiceover:

`بهالدرس ما بدنا نبلّش بالقسمة الطويلة. إذا كانت القسمة على (x - a)، أسرع حركة إنك تحسب P(a). وإذا طلعت P(a) = 0، فهاض يعني مباشرة إن (x - a) عامل.`

English subtitle:

`When dividing by (x - a), the fastest move is to evaluate P(a). If P(a) = 0, then (x - a) is a factor.`

Shot timeline:

1. `0-3s` Hook text with equation shell: `P(x) ÷ (x - a)`
2. `3-10s` Animate focus into `P(a)`
3. `10-18s` Show mini example: `P(1) = 0`
4. `18-24s` End card: `عوض أولاً ثم احكم` / `Substitute first, then decide`

### `u2-l1-hero.mp4`

Title:

- Arabic: `افهم المتطابقة قبل أن تحفظها`
- English subtitle: `See the identity before memorising it`

Core formula:

- `sin^2(theta) + cos^2(theta) = 1`
- `sin 2theta = 2 sin theta cos theta`

Arabic voiceover:

`المتطابقة ليست سطراً للحفظ فقط. على دائرة الوحدة نرى لماذا sin²θ + cos²θ = 1، ثم تصبح الصيغ أسرع وأوضح في البرهان.`

English subtitle:

`The identity is not just a line to memorise. On the unit circle, you can see why sin²θ + cos²θ = 1.`

Shot timeline:

1. `0-3s` Unit-circle hook
2. `3-10s` Show triangle on the circle and label `sin` and `cos`
3. `10-18s` Flash the identity and one quick simplification
4. `18-26s` End card: `ابدأ من الصورة ثم اكتب البرهان` / `Picture first, proof second`

### `u3-l3-hero.mp4`

Title:

- Arabic: `قاعدة السلسلة بدون تعقيد`
- English subtitle: `Chain rule without confusion`

Core formula:

- `d/dx[f(g(x))] = f'(g(x)) * g'(x)`

Arabic voiceover:

`في قاعدة السلسلة نشتق الخارج أولاً، ثم نضرب في مشتقة الداخل. خذ الدالة sin(3x²): مشتقة الخارج cos، ثم نضرب في 6x.`

English subtitle:

`Differentiate the outer function first, then multiply by the derivative of the inner function.`

Shot timeline:

1. `0-3s` Formula appears in layers
2. `3-10s` Highlight `outer` then `inner`
3. `10-18s` Example: `sin(3x^2)` -> `6x cos(3x^2)`
4. `18-25s` End card: `الخارج ثم الداخل` / `Outer, then inner`

### `u6-l1-hero.mp4`

Title:

- Arabic: `المتجهات في الفراغ بشكل واضح`
- English subtitle: `Vectors in space made clear`

Core formula:

- `|v| = sqrt(x^2 + y^2 + z^2)`
- `v_hat = v / |v|`

Arabic voiceover:

`المتجه في الفراغ لا يحتاج رهبة. نحسب المقدار من الجذور الثلاثة، ثم نحصل على متجه الوحدة بقسمة المتجه على مقداره.`

English subtitle:

`Find the magnitude first, then divide the vector by its magnitude to get the unit vector.`

Shot timeline:

1. `0-3s` 3D axes and vector arrow
2. `3-10s` Magnitude formula builds term by term
3. `10-18s` Example: `(3,4,0)` -> `|v| = 5`
4. `18-25s` End card: `المقدار ثم متجه الوحدة` / `Magnitude, then unit vector`
