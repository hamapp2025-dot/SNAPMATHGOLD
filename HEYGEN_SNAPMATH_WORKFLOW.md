# HeyGen Workflow For SnapMath Avatar Videos

This is the exact fastest workflow for making SnapMath Academy founder and lesson videos in `HeyGen`.

## What to make first

Make these in order:

1. `founder-intro.mp4`
2. `founder-welcome.mp4`
3. founder portrait still image
4. `u1-l1` avatar hook
5. `u2-l1` avatar hook

Do not start with all lessons at once.

## Part 1. Create your avatar in HeyGen

Use a custom avatar / digital twin of your face.

### Avatar setup target

Use this look:

- chest-up framing
- direct eye contact
- calm confident expression
- black, charcoal, or navy top
- dark clean background
- soft front light
- minimal hand movement

Do not use:

- bright colorful clothes
- wide smile
- exaggerated gestures
- fast hand movements
- busy office background

## Part 2. Voice setup

Best option:

- use your cloned voice if it sounds clean in Arabic

If the clone sounds weak:

- use the HeyGen voice that sounds closest to your tone
- keep Arabic as the spoken language

### Voice settings

Target voice style:

- calm
- teacher-like
- premium
- slightly slower than normal conversation

If HeyGen gives style controls, use:

- speed: `0.95`
- energy: medium
- expressiveness: medium-low
- pauses: natural

## Part 3. Founder intro video

## Canvas settings

- ratio: `9:16`
- export: `1080x1920`
- duration target: `6` to `10` seconds

## Scene setup

Use one clean scene only.

Background:

- dark premium studio
- or very dark gradient
- no busy furniture

Add only:

- subtle logo if needed
- one gold accent line if it looks clean

## Paste this script

Arabic spoken script:

`أنا حمزة الفساطلة. بنيت SnapMath Academy حتى يفهم طالب الأردن الرياضيات خطوة بخطوة ويحل بثقة في التوجيهي.`

### Better TTS punctuation version

Paste this if you want cleaner pacing:

`أنا حمزة الفساطلة. بنيت SnapMath Academy، حتى يفهم طالب الأردن الرياضيات خطوة بخطوة، ويحل بثقة في التوجيهي.`

### Subtitle

English subtitle:

`I built SnapMath Academy so Jordanian students can understand Tawjihi math step by step and solve with confidence.`

### Performance direction

Tell HeyGen or tune the scene toward:

- calm confidence
- direct connection
- not promotional
- mentor energy

### Best emphasis words

Pause or stress these:

- `طالب الأردن`
- `خطوة بخطوة`
- `بثقة`
- `التوجيهي`

## Part 5. Founder portrait

Fastest method:

1. export the founder intro video
2. take the best still frame
3. crop it square
4. save it as `founder-portrait.jpg`

Best frame:

- direct eye contact
- mouth closed
- slight smile
- shoulders visible

## Part 6. Lesson hooks in HeyGen

Do not make full lesson videos in HeyGen.

Use HeyGen only for:

- `3` to `5` second opening hook
- optional `2` to `3` second closing line

Then finish the actual lesson hero in `CapCut` using:

- equations
- text overlays
- math visuals
- optional Manim or Canva animations

## `u1-l1` hook

Arabic:

`بهالدرس رح نختصرها عليك: عوّض أول، وبعدها حدّد مباشرة إذا الناتج باقٍ أو إذا (x - a) عامل.`

English subtitle:

`In this lesson, we use the shortcut first: substitute, then decide the remainder or factor directly.`

## `u2-l1` hook

Arabic:

`لا تحفظ المتطابقة فقط. في هذا الدرس سنراها بصرياً أولاً ثم نستخدمها بثقة.`

English subtitle:

`Do not just memorise the identity. In this lesson, we see it visually first, then use it with confidence.`

## `u3-l3` hook

Arabic:

`قاعدة السلسلة تصبح سهلة عندما تفرّق بين الخارج والداخل بوضوح.`

English subtitle:

`The chain rule becomes easy when you clearly separate the outer function from the inner one.`

## `u6-l1` hook

Arabic:

`في هذا الدرس سنجعل المتجهات في الفراغ واضحة جداً: المقدار أولاً، ثم متجه الوحدة.`

English subtitle:

`In this lesson, vectors in space become simple: magnitude first, then the unit vector.`

## Part 7. CapCut assembly after HeyGen

After exporting the avatar clip from HeyGen:

1. open `CapCut`
2. create a `1080x1920` project
3. place the HeyGen avatar clip first
4. add equation slides after it
5. add short English subtitles
6. add soft movement only
7. export as `H.264 MP4`

## Lesson hero timing target

Use this structure:

1. `0-5s` avatar hook
2. `5-18s` visual math explanation
3. `18-28s` worked example
4. `28-35s` recap or practice push
