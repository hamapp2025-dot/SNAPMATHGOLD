# AI Avatar Video Creation Guide

This guide is for creating SnapMath Academy founder and lesson videos using an AI avatar clone of your face instead of filming your real face on camera.

## Best practical setup

Recommended stack:

- Avatar clone: `HeyGen` first choice
- Alternative: `Synthesia`
- Fast editing: `CapCut`
- Audio cleanup if needed: `Adobe Podcast` or `Audacity`
- Compression if needed: `HandBrake`

Why this setup:

- fastest path to polished founder videos
- easiest subtitle workflow
- clean vertical export for the app
- good enough quality for launch

## Core rule for SnapMath

Do not make the avatar talk through the whole lesson.

Best structure:

- founder avatar for the opening hook
- math visuals and equations for the main explanation
- optional founder avatar for the closing recap

This looks much more premium than a full talking-head avatar lesson.

## Founder avatar workflow

### Step 1. Create the digital twin

In your avatar tool, create a custom avatar that matches this style:

- chest-up framing
- eye-level camera
- calm confident expression
- direct eye contact
- subtle smile, not too wide
- black, charcoal, or navy clothing
- dark premium background or soft studio background
- soft front light
- very small hand movement

Avoid:

- exaggerated gestures
- fast blinking
- wide dramatic smiles
- side-angle head turns
- busy backgrounds
- colorful clothing

### Step 2. Create the voice

Best option:

- clone your voice if the tool supports it well

If voice cloning is weak:

- use a calm Arabic male voice close to your tone
- keep the pace slightly slower than normal speech

Voice settings:

- language: Arabic
- style: confident, warm, teacher-like
- pace: `0.9x` to `1.0x`
- energy: medium
- pauses: natural

## Founder intro video

### Goal

The first launch video should feel like:

- trust
- seriousness
- Jordan Grade 12 authority
- premium but personal

### Script

Use this Arabic line:

`أنا حمزة الفساطلة. بنيت SnapMath Academy حتى يفهم طالب الأردن الرياضيات خطوة بخطوة ويحل بثقة في التوجيهي.`

English subtitle:

`I built SnapMath Academy so Jordanian students can understand Tawjihi math step by step and solve with confidence.`

### Avatar performance direction

Use these settings or notes in the avatar tool:

- tone: warm and sure
- expression: calm confidence
- emphasis words:
  - `طالب الأردن`
  - `خطوة بخطوة`
  - `بثقة`
  - `التوجيهي`

### Visual setup

- aspect ratio: `9:16`
- resolution: `1080x1920`
- duration: `6` to `10` seconds
- subtitle position: above the bottom safe area
- one gold accent line or subtle logo is enough

### Prompt for scene look

Use this if the tool allows scene prompting:

`Jordanian math mentor in a premium dark studio, chest-up framing, calm confident expression, direct eye contact, soft gold accent light, subtle movement, clean luxury education style, mobile vertical composition`

## Founder portrait

You do not need a real camera photo if you want the avatar look everywhere.

You can create the founder portrait by:

1. exporting a high-quality still frame from the avatar intro video
2. or generating a square branded portrait from the avatar tool

Use this style:

- square crop
- face centered
- shoulders visible
- calm confident expression
- same outfit as the intro video
- same lighting style as the intro video

Prompt:

`Square portrait of a premium Jordanian math mentor avatar, direct eye contact, calm confident expression, clean dark studio background, black or navy outfit, soft gold accent lighting, luxury education brand style`

## Lesson hero workflow

## Best lesson structure

For each lesson hero video:

1. `3-5s` avatar hook
2. `12-20s` visual explanation with equations
3. `5-8s` worked example
4. `3-5s` closing recap or practice push

The avatar should appear only at the start and maybe the end.

## Easiest production stack

For lesson hero videos, use:

- avatar tool for the opening line
- `CapCut` or `Canva` for equation screens and animated text
- optional `Manim` only for the strongest two launch lessons

If you want the fastest launch, do not wait for full Manim production for all lessons.

### `u1-l1`

`بهالدرس رح نختصرها عليك: عوّض أول، وبعدها حدّد مباشرة إذا الناتج باقٍ أو إذا (x - a) عامل.`

English subtitle:

`In this lesson, we use the shortcut first: substitute, then decide the remainder or factor directly.`

### `u2-l1`

`Do not just memorise the identity. In this lesson, we see it visually first, then use it with confidence.`

### `u3-l3`

`The chain rule becomes easy when you clearly separate the outer function from the inner one.`

Visual section:

- highlight outer then inner

### `u6-l1`

Avatar hook Arabic:

`في هذا الدرس سنجعل المتجهات في الفراغ واضحة جداً: المقدار أولاً، ثم متجه الوحدة.`

`In this lesson, vectors in space become simple: magnitude first, then the unit vector.`

Visual section:

- vector arrow

## CapCut assembly workflow

For each lesson hero:

1. Render the avatar hook from your avatar tool.
2. Import the avatar clip into `CapCut`.
3. Add equation slides after it.
4. Add soft motion or zoom only.
5. Use short Arabic text with small English subtitles.
6. End with a recap card or practice push.
7. Export as `1080x1920 H.264 MP4`.

## Subtitle rule

Best launch format:

- Arabic voice
- English subtitles
- very short subtitle lines

Keep subtitles:

- centered
- not too low
- 1 to 2 short lines only

## Export settings

Use these for all founder and lesson clips:

- `1080x1920`
- `30fps`
- `H.264 MP4`
- bitrate roughly `8-12 Mbps`
- no heavy filters

If you are using an avatar clone:

- use equations and moving visuals for the math

That is what will make the result look premium instead of fake.
